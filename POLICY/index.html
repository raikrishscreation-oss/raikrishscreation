About Us

Who we are

RàíKrísh's Collection is an Indian online store for handmade jewellery and handcrafted accessories. Our range includes handmade jewellery, clay jewellery, handmade hair clips and hairpins, handmade lockets, floral-inspired accessories, and other handcrafted decorative and fashion pieces.

What we care about

  • Creativity: we enjoy designing pieces that feel fresh, personal, and expressive.
  • Handmade craftsmanship: our products are made by hand, not mass-produced.
  • Individual character: because each piece is handmade, each one has its own character.
  • Thoughtful presentation: we aim to pack and present every order with care.
  • Customer satisfaction: we want you to feel confident and happy with what you receive.
  • Transparent ordering and payment: you will know the price, the delivery charges, and the total before you confirm your order.

How ordering works

You can order through Instagram, WhatsApp, or our official website. Every order is confirmed with you on WhatsApp before it is processed. Payment is made online, and orders are shipped through India Post. You do not need to create an account or log in to a separate application to place an order.

Customized products

We accept requests for customized handmade products. Customized orders follow a few extra steps, including an advance payment, which are explained in our Customized Product Policy.

Questions?

If you need help or have a question about a policy, please reach us through the channels on our Contact & Complaint Policy page.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Please note

Shipping is handled through India Post, and all applicable delivery charges are payable by the customer. Delivery charges will be communicated before order confirmation and must be paid in full before dispatch.

Shipping partner

All orders are shipped through India Post.

Delivery charges

The customer is responsible for paying all applicable delivery charges. Delivery charges are separate from product prices unless an offer explicitly states otherwise, and they are shown separately on the bill or invoice. We will tell you the delivery charges before you confirm your order. We do not promise free shipping unless we announce a free-shipping offer.

Payment before dispatch

The complete payable amount, including delivery charges, must be paid before dispatch begins. We dispatch orders only after payment has been verified. For customized products, the remaining product balance and delivery charges must be paid before dispatch.

Your delivery details

Please give us an accurate delivery address, PIN code, and an active contact number. Postal staff may need to reach you, and incorrect details can cause delay, return, or non-delivery.

Dispatch time and delivery time are different

Dispatch time is the time we need to prepare your order and hand it over to India Post. Delivery time is the time India Post takes to carry the parcel to your address. We will share an estimated dispatch or delivery period only when we reasonably know it. We do not guarantee any specific dispatch or delivery date.

What can affect delivery

Actual delivery time may depend on your destination, postal service operations, weather, holidays, and other circumstances beyond our control.

Tracking

We will share a tracking number with you whenever one is available for your shipment.

Delayed, returned, or undelivered shipments

If your shipment is delayed, returned to us, or marked undelivered, please message us on WhatsApp with your order details and tracking number (if you have one). We will give reasonable help in tracking the shipment and resolving the delivery issue with India Post.

If a parcel comes back because of an incorrect or incomplete address or because the recipient could not be reached, we will discuss the options with you. Re-sending the parcel may need fresh delivery charges in that case.

Lost, damaged, or not-delivered orders

We do not accept responsibility for every delay caused by India Post, but we will not simply deny your rights if a parcel is lost, damaged, or not delivered. We will review each case fairly and, where appropriate, offer a suitable remedy as explained in the Return & Refund Policy. Your rights under applicable consumer law are not affected.

Cancellation Policy

In short

Ordinary cancellation is not available once dispatch begins. After customized production begins, cancellation because of a change of mind will ordinarily not be accepted. Your legal consumer rights remain protected.

How to request cancellation

Please send your cancellation request to us on WhatsApp. Include your name and order details so we can find your order quickly.

Check before you confirm

Please review the product details, customization specifications, delivery address, product price, and delivery charges carefully before you confirm an order. This helps avoid the need for cancellation.

The three stages

What we can do depends on the stage your order has reached.

A. Before production or dispatch begins

We may consider a cancellation request made before dispatch begins. For a customized product, this includes requests made before production begins. If we approve the cancellation, we will refund the amount you paid for the cancelled order, after verification.

B. After customized production begins

Customized production begins only after we have received and verified the 50% advance and the customization specifications have been confirmed. Once production has begun, a request made solely because you changed your mind will ordinarily not be accepted. The advance will generally not be refunded after production begins, subject to applicable consumer protection laws.

If we cannot fulfil the agreed customization, or the finished product is defective or materially different from what was agreed, you may still be entitled to a remedy. See the Customized Product Policy and the Return & Refund Policy.

C. After dispatch has begun

Once the dispatch process has started, ordinary cancellation requests will not be accepted. This does not affect your remedies if the product is damaged, defective, incorrect, materially different from what was agreed, lost, or not delivered. Please see the Return & Refund Policy.

For the purposes of this policy, the dispatch process begins when we start packing the order for handover to India Post.

Cancellation by us

If we are unable to fulfil an order (for example, because an item is unavailable), we will inform you on WhatsApp and arrange an appropriate remedy, which may include a refund of the amount you have paid for that order.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy removes or limits any consumer right that applies to you under Indian law. These restrictions do not apply to the extent that the law gives you a right to a remedy.

Return & Refund Policy

Your consumer rights are protected

This policy does not remove any right you have under applicable Indian consumer protection law, including for defective, incorrect, or materially misdescribed products and for orders that are not delivered.

Our care with your order

Our handmade products are prepared and packed with care. If something is not right when your order arrives, please tell us.

When to contact us

Please contact us on WhatsApp as soon as reasonably possible if you receive:

  • A damaged product
  • A defective product
  • An incorrect product
  • A product that is materially different from the agreed description or customization
  • A product whose colour is completely different from the colour shown in the product image
  • An order that has not been delivered

Photographs or video

To help us look into your complaint, we may ask for photographs, pictures of the packaging, or an unboxing video. These help our investigation, but they are not an absolute legal condition for every valid complaint. If you do not have such evidence, please still contact us and we will review your complaint fairly.

How we review a complaint

We will acknowledge your message, look at the order details and any evidence, and tell you our decision on WhatsApp. Depending on the case, a remedy may include a replacement, repair, or a refund. We will handle your complaint consistently with applicable law.

Returns are not accepted for change of mind

We do not accept returns because you changed your mind, for example if you no longer want the product or do not like it after delivery.

When we will offer a refund or replacement

A return, refund, or replacement will be considered if you receive:

  • A damaged product
  • A wrong product
  • A product whose colour is completely different from the colour shown in the product image

In these cases, we will arrange a refund or a replacement after reviewing your complaint. Small differences that come from handmade work or screen settings are explained in the Handmade Product Disclaimer and are not counted as a completely different colour.

This rule does not limit any right you have under applicable consumer law, including for defective or materially misdescribed products and for orders that are not delivered.

Delivery and return-shipping charges

If your complaint is valid (a damaged, wrong, defective, or materially different product, or a colour completely different from the product image), we will bear the reasonable cost of returning the item or sending a replacement, and the delivery charges you paid for the affected order will be considered in any refund. This never limits a remedy that applicable consumer law gives you.

Lost or not-delivered orders

If your order has not reached you, please message us on WhatsApp with your order details and tracking number (if available). We will help track the shipment with India Post. If the order is found to be lost or not delivered and this is not due to the customer, we will offer an appropriate remedy, such as sending the order again or a refund, in line with applicable law.

Product Care Policy

With good care, your handmade pieces will stay beautiful for longer. These are general guidelines for our jewellery, clay accessories, hair clips, lockets, and other handcrafted items. Please follow any product-specific care instructions we give you, as they take priority.

Small parts and children

Some pieces have small components that may be a choking hazard. Please keep them away from young children.

Keep products dry

Avoid water where the materials are not water-resistant. Remove jewellery before bathing, swimming, washing, or exercising, and wipe it gently with a dry soft cloth if it gets damp.

Handle gently

Avoid dropping, bending, pulling, or pressing the products with excessive force. Clay and other handmade materials can chip, crack, or break.

Avoid chemicals and heat

Keep products away from perfume, hair spray, lotions, cleaning products, and other chemicals. Where appropriate for the material, also keep them away from excessive heat and direct sunlight for long periods.

Wear them last

Put on your jewellery after applying makeup, perfume, and hair products.

Store carefully

Store each piece separately in a soft pouch, cloth, or box so that pieces do not rub, scratch, or knock against each other.

Hair clips and hairpins

Use hair clips and hairpins gently and avoid forcing them onto very thick hair, as this may damage the clip or the decoration.

Follow product-specific instructions

If we give care instructions for a particular product, please follow them.

Skin sensitivity

People's skin reacts differently to different materials. We do not claim that every product is waterproof, hypoallergenic, tarnish-proof, or suitable for every skin type. If you have sensitive skin, please ask us about the materials used before you buy, and stop wearing a piece if it irritates your skin.
